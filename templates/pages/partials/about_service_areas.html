Expert Services

Alongside pandit and temple listings, Nitya Dharma provides a platform to discover experts and explore the consultation services available through the site. Expert categories currently supported on the platform include:

  • Astrology: Browse astrologer listings and explore profiles for astrology-related guidance.
  • Numerology: Discover numerologists listed on the platform and review their public profile details.
  • Palmistry: Find palmistry experts and explore the information shared on their listings.

You can search and refine expert listings using tools available in the application, such as keyword search, minimum experience, minimum rating, and a verified-only filter where offered. Experts may join through the Expert registration flow; profiles are reviewed by Super Admin before they go live, helping maintain listing quality.

From an expert profile, visitors can review the details shared by the expert and use the platform’s existing booking-intent options to express interest. Availability, consultation formats, and fees depend on each individual listing.

Hindu Marriage Bureau

Nitya Dharma’s Hindu Marriage Bureau service helps individuals and families discover matrimonial connections in a culturally respectful environment by connecting them with listed Hindu Marriage Bureaus on the platform.

Through the Hindu Marriage Bureau listings you can:

  • Browse and search bureau profiles using city, caste filters, and keyword search where available.
  • Open a bureau profile to read the description and other details the bureau has shared.
  • Review ratings and feedback shown on bureau profiles when reviews are present.
  • Send a booking intent or use the contact options displayed on a bureau profile when the bureau has provided them.

Hindu Marriage Bureau owners can register on the platform, complete their profile details, and appear in public listings after the platform’s approval and activation steps. Listing content, contact preferences, and next steps are managed by each bureau. Nitya Dharma provides discovery and connection tools based on the information bureaus publish—it does not replace personal judgment or family decision-making.
