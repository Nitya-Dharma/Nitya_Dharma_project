## Summary

Task 17.7 defines REVIEW-RELATED NOTIFICATIONS without allowing retaliation, visibility bias, or trust manipulation. This policy document keeps review notifications strictly informational, prevents notifications from influencing trust or visibility, avoids creating response pressure or retaliation incentives, and maintains clear separation from Module 9 — Reviews.

## Core Principles Established

### Review & Interaction Notification Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Review notifications are awareness-only — no influence on trust or visibility.

**Review notifications inform — they do not influence.**

**Review notifications are awareness-only.**

**Review notifications are not trust or visibility tools.**

**Only explicit, completed actions trigger notifications.**

**Trust, visibility, and ranking are not notification triggers.**

**No trust or visibility implications.**

**No response pressure or retaliation incentives.**

**Trust and visibility remain authoritative elsewhere.**

**Reviewer and reviewed Pandit only — no observers.**

**Governance authority is absolute.**

**Review notifications respect module boundaries and remain neutral.**

---

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Review Notifications ARE:
- Informational alerts
- Awareness signals
- Event reflections
- Non-influential messages

### Review Notifications are NOT:
- Trust signals
- Visibility boosters
- Response prompts
- Reputation indicators
- Ranking influences

---

## Allowed Review Notification Triggers (LOCKED)

Users MAY receive notifications for:

### 1. New Review Posted
- A review was posted about a Pandit
- A review was posted by a user

### 2. Review Reported
- A review was reported
- Report status communicated

---

## Forbidden Review Notification Triggers (LOCKED)

Users must NEVER be notified for:
- Review rating changes
- Review visibility changes
- Trust score updates
- Reputation milestones
- Response requests
- Review moderation actions
- Ranking changes

---

## Neutrality & Anti-Retaliation Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Awareness-Only Principle:** Notifications describe what happened — nothing more.

2. **No-Response-Pressure Principle:** Notifications never imply obligation to respond.

3. **No-Trust-Influence Principle:** Notifications never influence trust scores or visibility.

4. **Participant-Only Principle:** Only direct participants receive review notifications.

5. **Governance Supremacy Principle:** Governance actions override all review notifications.
