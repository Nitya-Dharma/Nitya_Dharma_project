## Core Principles Established

### Page Content & Posting Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Pages announce — they do not negotiate.

**Page content is informational & broadcast-only.**

**Announcements are informational only.**

**Events are announcements, not bookings.**

**Text updates are broadcast-only.**

**Images are view-only.**

**No native video hosting logic here.**

**Pages are non-commercial and non-transactional.**

**No two-way interaction model exists.**

**Content is governed and monitored, not interactive.**

---

## Allowed Page Content Types (LOCKED)

### 1. Announcements
- Temple notices
- Pandit updates
- Schedule announcements (non-bookable)
- Informational messages

### 2. Events (Informational Only)
- Festival announcements
- Special pooja dates
- Temple events

**Events must NOT:**
- Accept registrations
- Accept payments
- Guarantee availability

### 3. Text Updates
- Plain text posts
- Religious messages
- Informational statements

### 4. Images (Display-Only)
- Temple photos
- Event posters
- Ceremonial images

### 5. Embedded Videos
- YouTube / Vimeo embeds
- Recorded discourses
- Informational video links

---

## Forbidden Content (LOCKED)

Pages must NEVER contain:
- Booking links or CTAs
- Payment or donation requests
- Reviews, ratings, or testimonials
- Trust claims ("verified", "best", "official")
- Contact forms or chat prompts
- External affiliate promotions
- Political or commercial ads

---

## Posting Authority Rules

**Only Page Owners (and future approved Page Admins) may:**
- Create posts
- Edit posts
- Remove posts

**Followers and visitors:**
- Cannot post
- Cannot comment
- Cannot react
- Cannot message
