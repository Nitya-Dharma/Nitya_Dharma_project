## Core Principles Established

### Page Future Expansion Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Readiness means "safe to extend later" — not "does more now."

**Compatibility is structural, not functional.**

**Compatibility is not pre-implementation.**

**Multi-admin requires new policy task.**

**Badges are display-only, not authority.**

**Calendars are announcements, not transactions.**

**Streams are one-way, not interactive.**

**All require explicit new module tasks.**

**If a future feature violates the permanently forbidden list, it must be rejected at design stage.**

**Expansion is opt-in, not automatic.**

**Pages remain non-authoritative.**

**Past content remains valid.**

**Future features remain isolated.**

**Pages remain consumers, never controllers.**

---

## Allowed Future Extensions (POLICY-ONLY)

The following MAY be added in the future, only by reopening policy, never silently:

### 1. Multi-admin Pages
- Additional Page Admin roles
- Still no followers with write access

### 2. Verified Page Badges
- Display-only
- No trust score
- No ranking influence

### 3. Event Calendars
- Informational only
- No booking or ticketing

### 4. Live Streams / Media Feeds
- Broadcast-only
- No chat, no payments

---

## Permanently Forbidden (LOCKED)

Pages must NEVER evolve into:
- Booking systems
- Payment or donation surfaces
- Review or rating platforms
- Trust certification systems
- Recommendation engines
- Messaging or chat systems
- Algorithmic ranking inputs

---

## Future-Safety Principles (LOCKED)

### 1. Opt-In Expansion Principle
- No future feature is automatic
- Every expansion requires: New task, New policy, Explicit approval

### 2. Authority Containment Principle
- Pages never gain authority
- Trust, governance, search remain external

### 3. Backward Compatibility Principle
- Existing Pages must not change meaning
- Old content remains valid and neutral

### 4. Isolation Preservation Principle
- Future Page features must not: Affect search ranking, Affect recommendations, Affect trust display, Affect analytics logic
