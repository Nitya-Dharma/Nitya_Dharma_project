## Summary

Task 11.4 establishes reporting rules for Pandit media in Module 11 — Governance, Moderation & Appeals. This policy document defines how media can be reported, who can report it, and what must never be allowed, ensuring media reporting is user-safe, abuse-resistant, human-reviewed, and non-destructive by default.

## Core Principles Established

### Reporting Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Reporting media raises a flag — it does not enforce action.

**Visibility control ≠ content judgment.**

Media reporting protects the platform without becoming censorship.

---

## What Media Can Be Reported (LOCKED)

Only the following Pandit-owned media may be reported:
- Profile images
- Gallery images
- Embedded videos (if supported)

**Key Rule:** Media must belong to a Pandit profile to be reportable.

---

## Who Can Report Media (LOCKED)

Only the following may report media:
- ✅ Logged-in registered users
- ✅ Logged-in Pandits
- ❌ Guests may NOT report
- ❌ Anonymous reporting is NOT allowed

---

## Valid Media Report Reasons (LOCKED)

Media may be reported ONLY for:
- Explicit or offensive content
- Abusive or hateful imagery
- Misleading or deceptive visuals
- Policy or legal violations
- Platform safety concerns

**Key Rule:** Reports must relate to policy violations, not dislike.

---

## Forbidden Reporting Uses (LOCKED)

Media reporting must NEVER be used for:
- ❌ Business competition
- ❌ Personal disputes
- ❌ Low ratings or reviews
- ❌ Trust or referral disputes
- ❌ Search visibility complaints

**Key Rule:** Media reporting cannot be weaponized.

---

## Scope Boundaries (LOCKED)

Media reporting must NEVER:
- Instantly remove media
- Auto-hide media permanently
- Affect Pandit ranking
- Affect trust display meaning
- Trigger blocks without review
- Modify search or SEO

---

## Media Reporting Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Human Review Principle:** All reported media requires manual governance review.

2. **Least-Impact Principle:** Temporary hiding (if any) must be reversible, logged, and appealable.

3. **Neutrality Principle:** Reporting does NOT imply guilt or wrongdoing.

---

## Module Boundary Alignment

- **Module 11 (Governance):** Reviews media reports
- **Module 4 (Profiles):** Media source only
- **Module 10 (Trust Display):** ❌ No trust impact
- **Module 7 (Search):** ❌ No ranking impact
- **Module 15 (Recommendations):** ❌ No impact

**Key Insight:** Media reporting never influences discovery systems.
