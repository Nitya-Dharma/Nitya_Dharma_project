## ML / AI Safety Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Backward Compatibility Principle:** Rule-based recommendations remain valid. ML cannot reinterpret past behavior.

2. **Explainability Principle:** Outputs must be explainable conceptually. No "black box" authority.

3. **Non-Escalation Principle:** ML does not elevate recommendations into decisions. ML does not imply quality or endorsement.

4. **Explicit Opt-In Principle:** ML introduction requires explicit module-level approval. Silent upgrades are forbidden.
