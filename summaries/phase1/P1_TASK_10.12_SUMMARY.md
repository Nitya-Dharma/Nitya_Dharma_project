## Core Principles Established

### Trust display without disclaimers creates:
- Legal risk
- Expectation risk
- False assumptions

### This task exists to:
- Align UX with system truth
- Prevent false assumptions
- Preserve platform neutrality

**Key Principle:** UX must never say more than the system can prove.

---

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Trust Disclaimers MUST:
- Clarify trust ≠ guarantee
- Clarify trust ≠ endorsement
- Clarify trust ≠ approval
- Clarify trust ≠ service outcome

### Trust Disclaimers MUST NOT:
- Be optional
- Be hidden contextually
- Change meaning across screens

---

## Required Disclaimer Concepts (LOCKED)

All trust display must communicate:
- Trust is informational only
- Trust does not guarantee service quality
- Platform does not endorse Pandits
- Trust indicators reflect limited facts

**Key Principle:** Clarity is mandatory, not optional.

---

## Hard Scope Boundaries (LOCKED)

Trust disclaimers must **NEVER:**
- Be omitted
- Be overridden by design
- Be replaced by icons alone
- Imply platform judgment
- Conflict with governance rules

---

## Design Principles

1. **Truth-Alignment Principle:** UX mirrors system reality, no exaggeration, no false implications
2. **Neutrality Principle:** No persuasion or influence, no platform endorsement, no quality claims
3. **Consistency Principle:** Same disclaimers everywhere, no context-dependent meaning, no screen-specific variations
