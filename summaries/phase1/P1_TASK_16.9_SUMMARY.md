## Core Principles Established

### Page Discovery & Search Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Pages may be found — never pushed.

**Discovery requires explicit user intent.**

**Discovery is not competitive or promotional.**

**Search results are explicit and separate.**

**Links require explicit user action.**

**Pages do not compete for visibility.**

**Search may index Pages, not optimize them.**

**Pages are indexed, not optimized.**

---

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Page Discovery IS:
- Passive visibility
- User-initiated
- Query-based
- Non-competitive
- Neutral

### Page Discovery is NOT:
- Ranking optimization
- Popularity comparison
- Authority signaling
- Trust elevation
- Recommendation logic

---

## Allowed Discovery Paths (LOCKED)

Pages MAY be discovered via:

### 1. Direct search
- Page name
- Temple name
- Pandit name
- Explicit Page search results
- Pages listed separately from Pandits
- No mixed ranking

### 2. Direct links
- Shared URLs
- External references

---

## Forbidden Discovery Behaviors

Pages must NEVER:
- Influence Pandit search ranking
- Appear as "top" or "featured"
- Be boosted by followers or posts
- Be ranked by engagement or views
- Be injected into recommendations
- Modify search relevance

---

**Module 7 (Search) controls:**
