## Security Incident — Formal Definition (LOCKED)

A security incident is any event that:
- Threatens confidentiality
- Threatens integrity
- Threatens availability
- Indicates abuse or compromise

This includes:
- Confirmed attacks
- Suspected breaches
- Anomalous system behavior

**Key Rule:** Suspicion alone is sufficient to trigger response discipline.

---

## Incident Response Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Containment-First Principle:** Stop further damage. Do not expand exposure. Avoid irreversible actions.

2. **Evidence Preservation Principle:** Preserve logs. Preserve state. Avoid destructive fixes. Fixing too early destroys learning.

3. **Human-Controlled Response Principle:** No auto-punishments. No automated bans. No irreversible actions without review.

4. **Least-Impact Principle:** Protect innocent users. Avoid broad shutdowns. Prefer scoped mitigation.

---

## Hard Response Boundaries (LOCKED)

**Incident response must NEVER:**
- ❌ Auto-block users
- ❌ Auto-ban Pandits
- ❌ Change governance outcomes
- ❌ Affect rankings or trust logic
- ❌ Leak incident details publicly

**Key Principle:** Security responds — governance decides.
