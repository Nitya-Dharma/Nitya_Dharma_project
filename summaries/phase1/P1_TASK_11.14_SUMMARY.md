## Core Principles Established

### Legal & Compliance Readiness Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Governance prepares for accountability — it does not replace legal authority.

**Readiness ≠ Enforcement**  
**Compliance ≠ Surveillance**

---

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

**Governance × Legal & Compliance Readiness IS:**
- A defensive preparedness layer
- A record-keeping and traceability framework
- A lawful response capability

**Governance × Legal & Compliance Readiness IS NOT:**
- A legal decision engine
- A monitoring system
- A policy interpretation layer
- A compliance automation system

---

## Readiness Capabilities (LOCKED)

**Governance MUST be able to:**
- Produce audit logs of governance actions
- Explain what action was taken and when
- Identify who made a governance decision
- Preserve records during disputes or requests
- Support lawful data access requests

**Key Rule:** Explainability, not prediction.

---

## Explicit Non-Capabilities (LOCKED)

**Governance must NEVER:**
- ❌ Interpret laws programmatically
- ❌ Decide legality of content
- ❌ Predict legal risk
- ❌ Auto-comply with requests
- ❌ Disclose data without human review
- ❌ Create compliance scores

**Key Rule:** Lawyers decide law. Governance preserves facts.

---

## Data Handling Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Minimality Principle:** Collect only what governance needs. No speculative data retention.

2. **Integrity Principle:** Logs must be tamper-resistant. No silent modification of records.

3. **Purpose Limitation Principle:** Governance data used only for dispute resolution, audit explanation, and lawful requests. No secondary use. No analytics reuse.

## Governance Readiness Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Accountability Without Control:** Explain actions. Do not enforce outcomes.

2. **Human-in-the-Loop Principle:** All disclosures reviewed manually.

3. **Neutral Platform Principle:** No endorsement, no judgment, no assumptions.
