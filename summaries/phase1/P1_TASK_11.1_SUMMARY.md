## Core Principles Established

### Governance IS:
- A human-driven authority system
- A policy enforcement layer
- A dispute & appeal resolution mechanism
- A safety and accountability framework

### Governance IS NOT:
- Business logic
- Search logic
- Trust computation
- SEO logic
- Recommendation intelligence
- Automation engine

**Key Principle:** Governance enforces rules — it does not invent meaning.

---

## Governance Responsibilities

### Governance is responsible for:
- Safety
- Fairness
- Rule enforcement
- Dispute resolution
- Accountability

### Governance is NOT responsible for:
- Optimizing the platform
- Ranking Pandits
- Promoting or demoting visibility automatically
- Generating trust scores
- Making irreversible decisions without appeal

---

## Hard Authority Boundaries (LOCKED)

### Governance MAY:
- Review reports
- Apply limited or full blocks
- Temporarily restrict visibility
- Notify affected Pandits
- Review and decide appeals
- Maintain audit trails

### Governance MUST NEVER:
- Modify search ranking formulas
- Influence recommendation engines
- Inject or alter SEO content
- Auto-approve or auto-reject Pandits
- Permanently punish without appeal
- Make decisions based on engagement metrics
- Override locked module contracts

---

## Governance Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Human-in-the-Loop Principle:** All governance decisions require human review. No auto-blocking, auto-banning, or auto-judgment.

2. **Appeal-First Fairness Principle:** Every negative governance action must be explainable, reversible, and appealable. No irreversible decisions by default.

3. **Least-Power Principle:** Governance uses the minimum action required to resolve an issue. Limited block before full block. Visibility restriction before removal.

4. **Neutral Platform Principle:** Governance remains platform-neutral. Does not endorse Pandits, certify Pandits, or guarantee services.

## Module Boundary Alignment

- **Module 9 (Reviews):** May moderate visibility, never edit content

**Key Insight:** Governance reacts to signals — it does not generate them.
