**Performance must never influence governance outcomes.**

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Performance × Governance Boundary
Performance optimizations MUST:
- Preserve governance authority
- Never replace moderation decisions
- Never hide governance actions
- Never bypass governance controls

**Key Rule:** Performance must never influence governance outcomes.

---

### Performance × Audit Boundary
Performance optimizations MUST:
- Preserve all audit trails
- Never remove audit data for performance
- Never compress audit records beyond recoverability
- Never make audit data inaccessible

**Key Rule:** Audit trails are non-negotiable, regardless of performance.

---

## Hard Boundaries (LOCKED)

### Performance MUST NEVER:
- ❌ Delete governance records for performance
- ❌ Archive governance actions without full recoverability
- ❌ Compress audit logs beyond readability
- ❌ Bypass governance checks for speed
- ❌ Replace moderation with automated cleanup

**Key Rule:** Performance never justifies governance compromise.

---

### Performance MAY ONLY:
- ✅ Optimize query performance (without changing results)
- ✅ Cache non-authoritative data
- ✅ Clean operational residue (with safeguards)
- ✅ Archive historical data (with full preservation)

**Key Rule:** Performance optimizations must preserve truth and accountability.

## Performance × Governance Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Authority Preservation Principle:** Governance authority is immutable, regardless of performance needs.

2. **Non-Substitution Principle:** Performance optimizations never replace governance decisions.

3. **Visibility Preservation Principle:** Governance actions remain visible and traceable.

4. **Isolation Principle:** Performance optimizations are invisible to governance logic.

---

## Performance × Audit Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Audit Immutability Principle:** Audit trails are permanent and non-negotiable.

2. **Recoverability Principle:** All audit data remains fully recoverable.

3. **Accessibility Principle:** Audit data remains accessible to governance (with appropriate controls).

4. **Non-Compression Principle:** Audit data is never compressed beyond recoverability.
