---

## Safety Threats in Scope (LOCKED)

Governance must be prepared to address:

1. **Spam & Misuse:**
   - Fake profiles
   - Repeated abuse
   - Manipulative listings

2. **Fake Pandits:**
   - Impersonation
   - False credentials
   - Identity misuse

3. **Coordinated Abuse:**
   - Mass reporting attacks
   - Harassment campaigns
   - Platform manipulation

**Key Rule:** Presence of risk ≠ guilt.

---

## Safety Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Human Judgment Principle:** No automated guilt. Manual review only.

2. **Presumption of Neutrality:** No assumption of wrongdoing. Evidence-based review.

3. **Proportional Response Principle:** Minimum necessary action. Limited block before full block.

4. **Appeal-First Safety Principle:** Every safety action is appealable. No irreversible defaults.
