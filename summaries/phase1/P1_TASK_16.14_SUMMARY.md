## Core Principles Established

### Page × Reviews & Ratings Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Pages communicate updates — they do not validate quality.

**Reviews are not part of Pages.**

**Pages do not validate service quality.**

**All review interaction belongs exclusively to Module 9.**

**Reviews are centralized in Module 9.**

**Pages are informational only.**

**No parallel review systems.**

**Reviews never flow through Pages.**

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Page × Reviews IS:
- Fully disallowed
- Explicitly out of scope
- Redirected to Pandit Profiles only

### Page × Reviews is NOT:
- A display surface
- A collection surface
- A summary surface

## Hard Boundaries (LOCKED)

Pages must NEVER:
- Collect user reviews
- Display ratings or stars
- Display review counts
- Show excerpts or testimonials
- Aggregate review sentiment
- Link to "top reviewed" claims

---

## Separation Principles (LOCKED)

### 1. Single Review Authority Principle

### 2. Neutrality Principle
- Pages do not persuade
- Pages do not validate service quality

### 3. Anti-Shadow-Surface Principle
- No alternative credibility surfaces
- No duplication of review meaning
