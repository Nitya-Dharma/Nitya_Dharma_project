## Core Principles Established

### Readiness Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Readiness means "able to adapt" — not "already compliant".

**Undocumented security cannot be defended.**

**Readiness ≠ certification.**

---

## Compliance-Ready Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Auditability Principle:** Actions are traceable. Decisions are explainable. Logs exist without exposing users.

2. **Least Exposure Principle:** Data exposure is minimized. Sensitive data is compartmentalized. Defaults favor privacy.

3. **Documentation-First Principle:** Security intent is documented. Behavior is explainable post-facto.

## Future-Safe Security Guarantees (LOCKED)

**The system guarantees:**
- Clear trust boundaries
- Backend-enforced controls
- Human-controlled governance
- Immutable audit trails

**The system does NOT guarantee:**

- Legal approval
- Regulatory coverage
