- **Appeal-first fairness** — Every action is appealable
- **Non-algorithmic** — Governance does not influence ranking, SEO, or recommendations
- **Fully isolated** — Governance boundaries are hard-locked
- **Enterprise-grade** — Ready for legal and compliance requirements

### Key Boundaries
- Governance may restrict visibility — it must never influence logic
- Governance enforces rules — it does not invent meaning
- Governance prepares for accountability — it does not replace legal authority
- Governance is invisible to algorithms
