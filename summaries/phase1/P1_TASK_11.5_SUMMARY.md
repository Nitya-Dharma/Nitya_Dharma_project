Task 11.5 establishes reporting rules for reviews in Module 11 — Governance, Moderation & Appeals. ## Core Principles Established

### Reporting Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Reviews are user speech — governance controls visibility only.

**Reporting reviews enables review — not control.**

---

## Who Can Report Reviews (LOCKED)

Only the following may report reviews:
- ✅ Pandits (about their own profile)
- ✅ Governance roles
- ❌ Users may NOT report their own reviews
- ❌ Third-party Pandits may NOT report

---

## Valid Review Report Reasons (LOCKED)

Reviews may be reported ONLY for:
- Abusive or hateful language
- Explicit or offensive content
- Clearly false claims (policy-defined)
- Spam or irrelevant content
- Platform rule violations

**Key Rule:** Disagreement ≠ reportable offense.

---

## Forbidden Review Report Uses (LOCKED)

Review reporting must NEVER be used for:
- ❌ Low ratings
- ❌ Negative opinions
- ❌ Competitive suppression
- ❌ Trust score manipulation
- ❌ Rating cleanup

**Key Rule:** Pandits cannot curate sentiment.

---

## Scope Boundaries (LOCKED)

Review reporting must NEVER:
- Delete reviews permanently
- Edit review text
- Change ratings
- Affect reviewer accounts automatically
- Influence search, trust, or recommendations

---

## Review Reporting Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Visibility-Only Principle:** Governance may hide reviews temporarily or restore reviews after review. No content rewriting allowed.

2. **Fair Speech Principle:** Negative feedback is allowed unless it violates policy.

3. **Appeal Protection Principle:** All moderation outcomes must be logged, explainable, and appealable.

---

## Module Boundary Alignment

- **Module 9 (Reviews):** Content source
- **Module 11 (Governance):** Visibility moderation
- **Module 10 (Trust Display):** ❌ No trust meaning change
- **Module 7 (Search):** ❌ No ranking impact
- **Module 15 (Recommendations):** ❌ No impact

**Key Insight:** Reviews never become governance metrics.
