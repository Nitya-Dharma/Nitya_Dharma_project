## Core Principles Established

### Appeal Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Appeals are a right, not a favor.

**No governance action is final without appeal access.**

---

## Appeal Eligibility (LOCKED)

**Appeals may be submitted by:**
- Pandits directly affected by governance actions

**Appeals are allowed for:**
- Limited blocks
- Full blocks
- Visibility restrictions

**Appeals are NOT applicable for:**
- ❌ User reviews
- ❌ Ratings
- ❌ Search ordering
- ❌ Trust display meaning

---

## Appeal Timing Rules (LOCKED)

- Appeals must be allowed after notification
- No forced waiting penalties
- No one-time-only restriction by default

**Key Rule:** Access to appeal must be reasonable and non-punitive.

---

## Forbidden Appeal Restrictions (LOCKED)

Governance must NEVER:
- ❌ Disable appeal access
- ❌ Require payment to appeal
- ❌ Penalize repeat appeals automatically
- ❌ Treat appeal as admission of guilt

---

## Appeal Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Right-to-Be-Heard Principle:** Every affected Pandit may appeal.

2. **Neutrality Principle:** Appeals reviewed independently. No bias from original decision.

3. **Non-Retaliation Principle:** Appealing must not worsen outcome.
