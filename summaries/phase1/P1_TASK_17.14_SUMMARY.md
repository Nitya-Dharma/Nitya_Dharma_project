## Core Principles Established

### Notification × Governance & Trust Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Notifications must never become a governance or trust signal.

**Notifications inform — they never influence trust or governance.**

**Notifications reflect governance — they do not influence it.**

**Notifications are not trust or governance tools.**

**Governance authority is absolute — notifications never override it.**

**Notifications reflect governance — nothing more.**

**Trust authority is absolute — notifications never override it.**

**Notifications reflect trust — nothing more.**

**Search and recommendation authority is absolute.**

**Notifications are read-only reflections.**

**Notifications are non-authoritative.**

**Notifications never override authority.**

**Clear separation prevents leakage.**

**Authority is absolute.**

**Notifications respect all authoritative module boundaries.**

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Notification × Governance & Trust IS:
- Informational reflection
- Decision communication
- Status awareness
- Non-authoritative delivery

### Notification × Governance & Trust is NOT:
- Trust signal
- Governance decision
- Visibility indicator
- Ranking influence
- Appeal mechanism

---

## Governance Boundaries (LOCKED)

### Notifications MUST NEVER:
- Influence governance decisions
- Affect appeal outcomes
- Replace governance dashboards
- Bypass governance processes
- Create governance logic

### Notifications MAY:
- Reflect governance decisions
- Communicate governance status
- Inform about governance actions

---

## Trust Boundaries (LOCKED)

### Notifications MUST NEVER:
- Influence trust scores
- Affect trust badges
- Impact trust display
- Create trust signals
- Bypass trust computation

### Notifications MAY:
- Inform about trust-related events
- Communicate trust status changes
- Reflect trust display updates

---

## Visibility & Ranking Boundaries (LOCKED)

### Notifications MUST NEVER:
- Influence search ranking
- Affect visibility
- Impact recommendations
- Create ranking signals
- Bypass search or recommendation logic

---

## Non-Authoritative Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Reflection-Only Principle:** Notifications reflect decisions — they do not make them.

2. **No-Influence Principle:** Notifications never influence trust, governance, or ranking.

3. **Authority-Preservation Principle:** All authority remains with authoritative modules.

4. **Separation Principle:** Notifications are strictly separated from authoritative logic.

5. **Governance Supremacy Principle:** Governance and trust authority override all notifications.
