## Core Principles Established

### Notifications Philosophy
**Key Principle:** Notification = awareness, not authority.

**Notifications mirror events — they do not extend them.**

**Notifications are informational only.**

**Notifications are passive and non-authoritative.**

**Delivery is the only function.**

**Events create notifications. Notifications do not create events.**

**Notifications are subordinate to all authoritative modules.**

**User respect is a core system invariant.**

**Audit and authority remain outside this module.**

**Notifications are consumers of system state, never controllers.**

---

## Formal Definition (LOCKED)

### Notifications ARE:
- Event-driven delivery messages
- Passive information carriers
- Contextual alerts generated by other modules
- Read-only reflections of system events

**Key Rule:** Notifications mirror events — they do not extend them.

---

### Notifications are NOT:
- Conversations
- Commands
- Guarantees
- Requests for action enforcement
- Approval or rejection signals
- A replacement for dashboards

**Key Rule:** Notifications are informational only.

---

## Hard Scope Boundaries (LOCKED)

Notifications must NEVER:
- Trigger state changes
- Auto-execute actions
- Confirm bookings or availability
- Escalate governance actions
- Modify trust or visibility
- Affect search ranking
- Affect recommendations
- Perform analytics-based decisions
- Replace dashboards or admin views

---

## Core Design Principles (LOCKED)

1. **Delivery-Only Principle:** Notifications deliver information. They do not process, decide, or enforce.

2. **Event-Owned Principle:** Notifications are generated only in response to events. Event ownership always belongs to the source module.

3. **Non-Authoritative Principle:** Notifications never override Governance (Module 11), Security (Module 12), Trust Display (Module 10), Search (Module 7), or Recommendations (Module 15).

4. **User-Respect Principle:** No spam, no dark patterns, no forced engagement, clear opt-out rules (defined later).

---

## Explicit Module Ownership (READ-ONLY)

Notifications may be triggered by, but never control:
- **Module 8 — Booking Intent:** Informational alerts only
- **Module 9 — Reviews:** Awareness of activity
- **Module 11 — Governance:** Status & decisions
- **Module 16 — Pages:** Page updates & events
- **Module 5 — Dashboards:** Visibility summaries

---

## Strict Exclusions (LOCKED)

Notifications must NOT:
- Store business state
- Replace inboxes or chats
- Include sensitive PII
- Include internal IDs
- Act as audit records
- Be used as proof of delivery
